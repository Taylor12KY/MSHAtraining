Training support tool—not an MSHA certification.

An MSHA-approved training plan and Part 48 instructor are still required. Hands-on MSA W65 practice and approximately 8 hours of mine-site-specific training must be completed under the approved plan.

Read full compliance and playback information

This classroom support and attention-tracking tool is built around topics in 30 CFR §§ 48.5 and 48.25. Computer-based methods must be described in the approved training plan. The tool supports sequential progression, quizzes, and documentation of classroom time; it does not replace required instruction or hands-on training.

Video playback: If a required player is blocked by company Wi-Fi or a content filter, contact the instructor. Viewing through an external video link does not receive completion credit.
