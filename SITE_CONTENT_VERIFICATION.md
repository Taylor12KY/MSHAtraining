# Site Content Verification Checklist

The app intentionally does not invent emergency details. Before classroom use, an MSHA-approved instructor or designated site authority should compare Module 1 with the current approved plans for each mine.

## Verify for every site

- Current mine name, Mine ID, address, ownership, and operating status
- Current supervisor and foreman names; prefer role titles if personnel change frequently
- Active levels, mining method, material flow, crushers, conveyors, plants, and stockpile locations
- Check-in/check-out and tag-in/tag-out procedures
- Required PPE and self-rescuer type, inspection, carrying, and storage requirements
- Primary and secondary escapeways from each active work area
- Emergency gathering points and accountability procedure
- Radio channels, emergency contacts, alarm signals, and help-summoning procedure
- Fire response, extinguisher locations, evacuation expectations, and drill procedures
- Current ground-control, ventilation, escape-and-evacuation, and fire-plan terminology
- Traffic routes, restricted areas, blasting signals, and site-specific hazards

## Boonesboro Quarry

- Confirm Levels 2 and 3 remain the active production levels.
- Confirm Level 2 primary-crusher location, Level 1 shop/stockpile use, BT3 route, and Liwell plant description.
- Confirm the named supervisors or replace names with durable role titles.

## Clover Bottom Quarry

- Confirm Mine IDs 15-00112 and 15-19898 and the old-mine/new-mine distinction.
- Confirm floor mining, header mining, surface drill-and-blast, pit crusher, secondary plant, and Liwell plant descriptions.
- Verify separate escapeway maps and accountability procedures for both mines.
- Confirm the named supervisors or replace names with durable role titles.

## Dix River Stone

- Confirm Mine ID 15-18389, active mining on Levels 1 and 2, and whether surface mining remains inactive.
- Confirm surface primary crusher, surge silo, secondary plant, and underground/surface stockpile descriptions.
- Confirm the named supervisor or replace the name with a durable role title.

Record the plan revision dates and approving instructor beside this checklist before release. Do not store sensitive maps, phone numbers, or detailed security information in this public repository.
